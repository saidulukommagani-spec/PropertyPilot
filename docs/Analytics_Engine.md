# PropertyPilot Analytics Engine

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Analytics Engine provides centralized reporting, dashboards, KPIs, business intelligence, operational insights, and future predictive analytics across the PropertyPilot platform.

The Analytics Engine enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, operational efficiency, customer behavior, service quality, revenue growth, and platform adoption.

The Analytics Engine serves as the single source of truth for business reporting and decision-making.

# Business Rules

1. Every major business event shall be available for analytics.

2. Analytics shall support configurable KPIs.

3. Dashboard visibility shall be role-based.

4. Reports shall support filtering.

5. Analytics shall support exports.

6. Analytics activities shall be audit logged.

7. Analytics rules shall be configurable.

8. Analytics shall support future AI capabilities.

9. Analytics shall integrate with all major PropertyPilot modules.

10. Analytics Engine shall serve as the central business intelligence platform of PropertyPilot.
