# PropertyPilot Product Catalog

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Product Catalog defines all products, packages, subscriptions, add-ons, and marketplace offerings available within the PropertyPilot platform.

The Product Catalog serves as the central repository for all customer-facing offerings and acts as the foundation for pricing, booking, subscriptions, marketplace services, and future service expansion.

---

# Objectives

The Product Catalog shall:

- Standardize all platform offerings
- Support configurable service packages
- Support subscriptions
- Support add-on services
- Support marketplace offerings
- Support future construction and development services
- Integrate with Pricing Engine
- Integrate with ETA Engine
- Integrate with Service Workflow
- Support future scalability

# Business Rules

1. Every service offering shall be represented as a product.

2. Products shall belong to categories.

3. Products may support add-ons.

4. Products may support subscriptions.

5. Products shall integrate with Pricing Engine.

6. Products shall integrate with ETA Engine.

7. Products shall integrate with Service Workflow.

8. Product configuration shall not require code deployment.

9. Product visibility shall be configurable.

10. Product Catalog shall support future marketplace and development services.
