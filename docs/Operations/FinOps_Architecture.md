## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The FinOps Architecture defines the governance, processes, controls, accountability models, cost optimization strategies, financial visibility mechanisms, and operational practices required to manage cloud and technology spending across the PropertyPilot platform.

The FinOps framework enables PropertyPilot to maximize business value while maintaining financial accountability and operational efficiency.

---

# Objectives

The FinOps Architecture shall:

- Improve cloud cost visibility
- Improve financial accountability
- Optimize resource utilization
- Reduce waste
- Improve budgeting accuracy
- Support growth forecasting
- Enable chargeback and showback
- Improve cost governance
- Support investment decisions
- Align technology spending with business value

---

# Business Rules

1. All cloud resources shall be tagged according to FinOps standards.

2. Budget ownership shall be assigned for all major cost centers.

3. Cost reviews shall be conducted monthly.

4. Optimization opportunities shall be continuously identified.

5. Cost allocation shall be traceable and auditable.

6. Forecasts shall be reviewed quarterly.

7. Cost alerts shall be configured for budget thresholds.

8. Resource utilization shall be monitored continuously.

9. FinOps activities shall be audit logged.

10. FinOps Architecture shall serve as the authoritative framework for technology financial management within PropertyPilot.
