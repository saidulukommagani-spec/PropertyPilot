# PropertyPilot Capacity Planning

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Capacity Planning module defines the strategy, processes, governance, forecasting models, monitoring mechanisms, and operational controls required to ensure PropertyPilot has sufficient capacity to meet current and future business demands.

The document serves as the authoritative framework for infrastructure, application, database, storage, network, and operational capacity planning.

---

# Objectives

The Capacity Planning module shall:

- Ensure platform scalability
- Prevent capacity shortages
- Support business growth
- Improve performance
- Optimize infrastructure utilization
- Improve forecasting accuracy
- Support operational readiness
- Reduce performance risks
- Improve cost efficiency
- Enable proactive planning

---

- Data Driven

# Business Rules

1. Capacity planning shall be performed proactively.

2. Capacity reviews shall occur at least quarterly.

3. Critical capacity thresholds shall trigger alerts.

4. Growth forecasts shall be incorporated into planning.

5. Capacity risks shall be documented and mitigated.

6. Capacity activities shall be audit logged.

7. Scalability requirements shall be reviewed for new solutions.

8. Infrastructure investments shall be justified through forecasts.

9. Capacity metrics shall be continuously monitored.

10. Capacity Planning shall serve as the authoritative framework for scalability and growth management within PropertyPilot.
