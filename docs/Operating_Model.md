# PropertyPilot Operating Model

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the PropertyPilot Enterprise Operating Model.

The Operating Model describes:

- How business capabilities are delivered
- How teams are organized
- How domains are governed
- How technology is managed
- How services are operated
- How value is delivered to customers

---

# Operating Model Overview

PropertyPilot adopts a:

```text
Domain-Oriented
Product-Centric
Platform-Enabled
Cloud-Native
Data-Driven
Operating Model
```

---

# Operating Model Principles

## OM-001

Business capabilities drive technology investments.

---

## OM-002

Domains own business outcomes.

---

## OM-003

Teams own services end-to-end.

---

## OM-004

APIs and events are the preferred integration mechanisms.

---

## OM-005

Automation is preferred over manual processes.

---

## OM-006

Security is built into every lifecycle stage.

---

## OM-007

Data is a strategic enterprise asset.
