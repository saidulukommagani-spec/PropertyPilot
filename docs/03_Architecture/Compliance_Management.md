## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Compliance Management module enables PropertyPilot to define, monitor, enforce, audit, and improve compliance across business operations, services, vendors, agents, partners, franchises, security controls, and regulatory requirements.

The module serves as the centralized governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) framework for the entire PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Compliance Management module shall:

- Manage compliance requirements
- Monitor compliance obligations
- Support regulatory compliance
- Support operational compliance
- Support policy compliance
- Support compliance audits
- Support corrective actions
- Support compliance reporting
- Support risk mitigation
- Support future automated compliance monitoring

# Business Rules

1. Every compliance requirement shall have a unique Compliance ID.

2. Compliance controls shall be measurable.

3. Compliance reviews shall be scheduled and tracked.

4. Non-compliance events shall be recorded.

5. Corrective actions shall be auditable.

6. Compliance exceptions shall require approval.

7. Compliance activities shall be audit logged.

8. Compliance visibility shall be role-based.

9. Compliance configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Compliance Management shall serve as the centralized governance, risk, and compliance framework of PropertyPilot.
