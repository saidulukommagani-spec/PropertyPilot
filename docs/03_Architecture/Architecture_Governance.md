# Purpose

The Architecture Governance module defines the policies, standards, processes, controls, organizational structures, and decision-making mechanisms required to govern the PropertyPilot enterprise architecture.

The module serves as the authoritative governance framework ensuring that all architecture decisions align with business objectives, technology standards, security requirements, operational goals, and the PropertyPilot Reference Architecture.

---

# Objectives

The Architecture Governance module shall:

- Govern architecture decisions
- Enforce architecture standards
- Manage architecture reviews
- Manage architecture compliance
- Control technology exceptions
- Reduce architecture risk
- Improve solution consistency
- Support strategic planning
- Ensure regulatory alignment
- Support enterprise scalability

---

# Governance Principles

PropertyPilot architecture governance shall be:

- Business Driven
- Standards Based
- Security First
- Risk Aware
- Transparent
- Auditable
- Scalable
- Technology Neutral
- Compliance Focused
- Continuously Improved

# Business Rules

1. All architecture decisions shall align with the Reference Architecture.

2. Major solution designs shall undergo Architecture Review Board approval.

3. Technology exceptions shall require documented approval.

4. Architecture standards shall be centrally governed.

5. Architecture reviews shall be audit logged.

6. Security reviews shall be mandatory for critical solutions.

7. Architecture compliance shall be measured continuously.

8. Architecture artifacts shall be version controlled.

9. Governance policies shall be reviewed annually.

10. Architecture Governance shall serve as the authoritative governance framework for all PropertyPilot architecture decisions.
