# PropertyPilot Partner Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Partner Management module enables PropertyPilot to onboard, manage, verify, monitor, evaluate, and reward business partners who contribute to customer acquisition, lead generation, service sales, subscriptions, marketplace growth, and strategic business expansion.

The module serves as the central platform for managing partner relationships, agreements, commissions, performance, and partner-driven business activities.

---

# Objectives

The Partner Management module shall:

- Manage partner onboarding
- Support partner verification
- Support partner categorization
- Track partner-generated leads
- Track partner-generated revenue
- Support commission management
- Support partner performance tracking
- Support partner payouts
- Support agreement management
- Support future franchise operations

# Business Rules

1. Every partner shall have a unique Partner ID.

2. Partner verification levels shall be supported.

3. Partner-generated leads shall be traceable.

4. Commission calculations shall be auditable.

5. Partner agreements shall be maintained digitally.

6. Partner payouts shall be integrated with the Payment Engine.

7. Partner performance shall be measurable.

8. All partner activities shall be audit logged.

9. Partner configurations shall not require code deployment.

10. Partner Management shall serve as the business growth and partnership engine of PropertyPilot.
