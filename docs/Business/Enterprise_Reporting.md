# PropertyPilot Enterprise Reporting

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Reporting module enables PropertyPilot to generate, manage, distribute, schedule, analyze, and govern operational, financial, customer, project, compliance, and executive reports across the organization.

The module serves as the centralized reporting platform for decision-making, regulatory compliance, operational monitoring, and business intelligence.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Reporting module shall:

- Generate enterprise reports
- Support operational reporting
- Support executive reporting
- Support scheduled reporting
- Support report distribution
- Support report governance
- Support report security
- Support report analytics
- Support report auditing
- Support future AI-generated reports

# Business Rules

1. Every report shall have a unique Report ID.

2. Reports shall support configurable templates.

3. Report schedules shall be configurable.

4. Report access shall be role-based.

5. Report classifications shall be enforced.

6. Report activities shall be audit logged.

7. Report versions shall be maintained.

8. Report retention policies shall be configurable.

9. Report generation shall not require code deployment.

10. Enterprise Reporting shall serve as the centralized reporting and information delivery platform of PropertyPilot.
