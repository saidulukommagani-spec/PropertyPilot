# PropertyPilot Lead Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Lead Management module enables PropertyPilot to capture, qualify, assign, nurture, convert, and track leads generated through various channels including website inquiries, referrals, marketing campaigns, marketplace activities, subscriptions, and partner networks.

The module serves as the centralized lead acquisition and conversion engine for the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Lead Management module shall:

- Capture leads from multiple sources

- Support lead qualification

- Support lead assignment

- Support lead nurturing

- Support lead scoring

- Support lead conversion tracking

- Support follow-up management

- Support marketing campaign integration

- Support analytics and reporting

- Support future AI lead scoring

# Business Rules

1. Every lead shall have a unique Lead ID.

2. Lead qualification rules shall be configurable.

3. Lead assignment rules shall be configurable.

4. Lead scoring shall be supported.

5. Lead conversion shall be measurable.

6. Follow-up activities shall be tracked.

7. Lead analytics shall be available.

8. Lead activities shall be audit logged.

9. Lead configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Lead Management shall serve as the centralized lead acquisition and conversion engine of PropertyPilot.
