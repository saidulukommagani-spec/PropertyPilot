# PropertyPilot Customer Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Customer Management module serves as the master system for managing customers across the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

The module enables customer onboarding, profile management, KYC verification, communication preferences, subscriptions, service history, marketplace participation, payments, complaints, and customer engagement.

Every customer interaction within PropertyPilot shall be linked to a customer profile.
# Business Rules

1. Every customer shall have a unique Customer ID.

2. Every service request shall belong to a customer.

3. Customer verification rules shall be configurable.

4. Customer preferences shall be configurable.

5. Customer activities shall be audit logged.

6. Customer data access shall be role-based.

7. Customers may own multiple properties.

8. Customer communication history shall be maintained.

9. Customer configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Customer Management shall serve as the master customer repository for PropertyPilot.
