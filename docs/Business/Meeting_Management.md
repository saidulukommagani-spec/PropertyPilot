# PropertyPilot Meeting Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Meeting Management module enables PropertyPilot to schedule, organize, conduct, document, track, and analyze meetings across customers, agents, vendors, partners, franchises, projects, operations teams, and management.

The module serves as the centralized platform for meeting planning, attendance management, action item tracking, meeting documentation, and decision governance.

---

# Objectives

The Meeting Management module shall:

- Manage meeting scheduling
- Support meeting invitations
- Support attendance tracking
- Support meeting minutes
- Support action item tracking
- Support recurring meetings
- Support meeting analytics
- Support meeting documentation
- Support decision tracking
- Support future AI meeting assistance

# Business Rules

1. Every meeting shall have a unique Meeting ID.

2. Meetings shall support attendance tracking.

3. Meeting minutes shall be stored and searchable.

4. Action items shall be assignable and trackable.

5. Recurring meetings shall be supported.

6. Meeting activities shall be audit logged.

7. Meeting visibility shall be role-based.

8. Meeting documents shall be centrally managed.

9. Meeting configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Meeting Management shall serve as the centralized meeting governance and collaboration platform of PropertyPilot.
