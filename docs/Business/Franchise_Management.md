# PropertyPilot Franchise Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Franchise Management module enables PropertyPilot to onboard, manage, monitor, support, evaluate, and grow franchise partners who operate PropertyPilot services within assigned territories.

The module serves as the centralized platform for franchise operations, territory management, revenue sharing, compliance monitoring, performance evaluation, and business expansion.

---

# Objectives

The Franchise Management module shall:

- Support franchise onboarding
- Manage franchise territories
- Support franchise agreements
- Track franchise performance
- Support revenue sharing
- Support operational compliance
- Support franchise audits
- Support franchise expansion
- Support franchise analytics
- Support future multi-region operations

# Business Rules

1. Every franchise shall have a unique Franchise ID.

2. Franchise territories shall be managed centrally.

3. Franchise agreements shall be digitally maintained.

4. Revenue sharing models shall be configurable.

5. Franchise performance shall be measurable.

6. Franchise compliance shall be monitored.

7. Franchise activities shall be audit logged.

8. Franchise visibility shall be role-based.

9. Franchise configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Franchise Management shall serve as the business expansion and territory operations engine of PropertyPilot.
