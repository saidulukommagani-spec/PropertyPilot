1. Screen Design Philosophy
PropertyPilot is a Property-Centric Platform.
Every major activity should originate from a property.
Customer
    ↓
Property
    ↓
Services
    ↓
Lead
    ↓
Deal

Design Principles
- Property First
- Customer Focused
- Minimal Clicks
- Mobile Friendly
- Evidence Driven
- Role Based Access
- Enterprise Grade Navigation
- Consistent Screen Layouts
