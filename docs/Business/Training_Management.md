## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Training Management module enables PropertyPilot to plan, create, assign, deliver, track, evaluate, and certify training programs for agents, vendors, partners, franchises, employees, and operational teams.

The module serves as the centralized learning and capability development platform for ensuring consistent service quality, operational excellence, compliance, and business growth.

---

# Objectives

The Training Management module shall:

- Manage training programs
- Support learning paths
- Support certifications
- Support assessments
- Support onboarding training
- Support compliance training
- Support skill development
- Support training analytics
- Support role-based learning
- Support future AI-powered learning

# Business Rules

1. Every training program shall have a unique Training ID.

2. Training content shall support version management.

3. Training assignments shall be role-based.

4. Assessments shall support configurable scoring.

5. Certifications may have expiry dates.

6. Training completion shall be measurable.

7. Training activities shall be audit logged.

8. Training visibility shall be role-based.

9. Training configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Training Management shall serve as the centralized learning and certification platform of PropertyPilot.
