## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Business Intelligence (BI) module enables PropertyPilot to transform operational, financial, customer, service, vendor, project, marketplace, and compliance data into actionable business insights.

The module serves as the centralized intelligence and decision-support platform for management, operations teams, franchises, partners, and executives.

---

# Objectives

The Business Intelligence module shall:

- Provide business insights
- Support executive dashboards
- Support KPI tracking
- Support trend analysis
- Support forecasting
- Support operational intelligence
- Support financial intelligence
- Support customer intelligence
- Support performance analytics
- Support future AI-driven insights

# Business Rules

1. Every KPI shall have a unique KPI ID.

2. Dashboards shall support role-based visibility.

3. Reports shall be exportable.

4. KPI calculations shall be configurable.

5. Forecasting models shall support future enhancements.

6. Analytics activities shall be audit logged.

7. BI data shall support drill-down capabilities.

8. Business metrics shall be traceable to source systems.

9. BI configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Business Intelligence shall serve as the centralized analytics and executive decision-support platform of PropertyPilot.
