# PropertyPilot Campaign Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Campaign Management module enables PropertyPilot to plan, execute, monitor, optimize, and analyze marketing, sales, customer engagement, referral, and promotional campaigns.

The module serves as the centralized platform for customer acquisition, lead generation, customer retention, partner promotions, subscription growth, and marketplace expansion.

---

# Objectives

The Campaign Management module shall:

- Manage marketing campaigns
- Support lead generation campaigns
- Support referral campaigns
- Support partner campaigns
- Support customer engagement campaigns
- Support subscription promotions
- Support marketplace promotions
- Support campaign analytics
- Support campaign automation
- Support future AI campaign optimization

# Business Rules

1. Every campaign shall have a unique Campaign ID.

2. Campaigns shall support configurable workflows.

3. Audience segmentation shall be configurable.

4. Campaign budgets shall be trackable.

5. Campaign performance shall be measurable.

6. Revenue attribution shall be supported.

7. Campaign activities shall be audit logged.

8. Campaign visibility shall be role-based.

9. Campaign configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Campaign Management shall serve as the centralized marketing and growth engine of PropertyPilot.
