# PropertyPilot Contract Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Contract Management module enables PropertyPilot to create, negotiate, approve, execute, monitor, renew, and archive contracts and agreements across customers, vendors, partners, franchises, employees, and service providers.

The module serves as the centralized repository for all contractual relationships and legal obligations within the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Contract Management module shall:

- Manage contract lifecycle
- Support contract approvals
- Support contract renewals
- Support contract compliance
- Support contract versioning
- Support digital agreements
- Support obligation tracking
- Support expiry monitoring
- Support contract analytics
- Support future e-signature integrations

# Business Rules

1. Every contract shall have a unique Contract ID.

2. Contracts shall support version management.

3. Contract approvals shall be auditable.

4. Contract obligations shall be trackable.

5. Contract renewals shall be monitored.

6. Contract compliance shall be measurable.

7. Contract activities shall be audit logged.

8. Contract visibility shall be role-based.

9. Contract configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Contract Management shall serve as the centralized contract governance and agreement management platform of PropertyPilot.
