Subscription prices shown in this document are placeholder values.

Actual pricing shall be generated dynamically by the Pricing Engine based on:

- Property Type
- Property Size
- Location
- Distance
- Travel Time
- Service Frequency
- Included Services
- Vendor Cost
- Market Conditions

The subscription plans define service entitlements and benefits.

The Pricing Engine determines final customer pricing.

# Additional Plans

## Construction Monitoring Plan

For property owners requiring recurring visibility during construction. The plan may include configured construction-monitoring visits, progress evidence, reports, and escalation support drawn from the Construction Services and NRI Property Services catalog categories. Project-execution work remains separately quoted where applicable.

## Agriculture Monitoring Plan

For agricultural-land owners requiring recurring monitoring. The plan may include configured crop monitoring, crop-health inspection, GPS and photo evidence, status reporting, and applicable agriculture service add-ons. Eligibility depends on property type, coverage, documentation, and service availability.

## Rental Property Plan

For owners who require recurring rental-property oversight. The plan may include configured periodic inspections, tenant-verification or rental-inspection services, reports, alerts, and rental-management support. Tenant search, agreement assistance, and other non-included services remain separately quoted.

## Commercial Property Plan

For commercial-property owners requiring ongoing inspection and monitoring. The plan may include configured commercial inspections, occupancy or facility-management inspection, status reporting, evidence, and priority support. Specialist verification and execution services remain subject to separate eligibility and pricing rules.

# Additional Add-ons

## Live Video Inspection

Eligible subscribers may add a live video inspection or walkthrough to a scheduled or one-time visit. The add-on shall be separately itemised and subject to location, agent availability, and plan entitlement rules.

## Virtual Evidence Package

Eligible subscribers may select a configurable package of 360-degree virtual tour, professional photography, professional videography, drone photography, or drone videography. Each selected component shall remain visible in the quote and subject to applicable skill, licence, coverage, and vendor or agent availability.

## Same-Day Service

Eligible subscribers may request same-day service where the selected catalog service, coverage area, and available capacity support it. Any premium or plan benefit shall be calculated by the Pricing Engine at checkout.

## Weekend Service

Eligible subscribers may request weekend service for services configured as available on weekends. The quote shall apply the active weekend, subscription-benefit, and discount rules without changing the underlying plan entitlement.

# Additional Subscription Rules

1. A subscription shall be linked to its enrolled property or properties and configured visit frequency. The plan configuration must state the property scope of each entitlement before activation.

2. Before a plan or add-on is offered for checkout, the system shall validate property type, coverage, cluster, required documentation, customer verification, and service availability.

3. Included visits shall be consumed against the active plan entitlement. Excess visits, non-included services, and non-entitled add-ons shall receive a separate, itemised quote under the Pricing Strategy.

4. A subscription quote shall identify the pricing-rule version and remain valid only for its configured validity period. Renewal pricing shall be recalculated under the active rules and presented before renewal confirmation.

5. Plan and entitlement changes shall be effective dated. They shall not retrospectively alter an active paid term, except where an approved governing policy explicitly permits the change.

6. Subscription benefits, promotions, coupons, and emergency-visit discounts shall apply only when their eligibility, scope, validity, usage limits, and stackability rules are satisfied. The same benefit shall not be applied more than once to a quote.
