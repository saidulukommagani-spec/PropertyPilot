## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Project Management module enables PropertyPilot to plan, execute, monitor, control, and complete long-term customer projects such as property development, compound wall construction, farm development, borewell execution, fencing, land leveling, infrastructure works, and custom development services.

The module serves as the centralized execution platform for project-based services delivered by PropertyPilot, vendors, partners, and franchise operators.

---

# Objectives

The Project Management module shall:

- Manage project lifecycle
- Support project planning
- Support project execution
- Support milestone tracking
- Support project budgeting
- Support resource allocation
- Support vendor coordination
- Support project analytics
- Support project governance
- Support future AI project optimization

# Business Rules

1. Every project shall have a unique Project ID.

2. Projects shall support configurable workflows.

3. Projects shall support milestone management.

4. Budget and cost tracking shall be supported.

5. Resource allocation shall be tracked.

6. Risks and issues shall be managed.

7. Project activities shall be audit logged.

8. Project visibility shall be role-based.

9. Project configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Project Management shall serve as the centralized execution and delivery management platform of PropertyPilot.
