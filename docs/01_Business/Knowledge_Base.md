## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Knowledge Base module serves as the centralized repository for organizational knowledge, standard operating procedures (SOPs), training materials, FAQs, troubleshooting guides, business policies, compliance documents, and operational best practices.

The module enables PropertyPilot employees, agents, vendors, partners, and customers to access accurate, structured, and searchable knowledge.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Base module shall:

- Centralize organizational knowledge
- Support SOP management
- Support training materials
- Support FAQs
- Support troubleshooting guides
- Support policy management
- Support document versioning
- Support knowledge search
- Support role-based access
- Support future AI-powered knowledge assistance

# Business Rules

1. Every knowledge asset shall have a unique Knowledge ID.

2. Knowledge articles shall support version management.

3. Knowledge visibility shall be role-based.

4. Knowledge approval workflows shall be configurable.

5. Knowledge search shall be supported.

6. Knowledge feedback shall be captured.

7. Knowledge activities shall be audit logged.

8. Knowledge configuration shall not require code deployment.

9. Knowledge content shall support future AI integrations.

10. Knowledge Base shall serve as the centralized organizational knowledge repository of PropertyPilot.
