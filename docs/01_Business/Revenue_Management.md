# PropertyPilot Revenue Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Revenue Management module enables PropertyPilot to track, manage, analyze, forecast, and optimize all revenue generated across the platform.

The module serves as the central financial revenue repository for service revenue, subscriptions, marketplace revenue, commissions, partner revenue, vendor revenue sharing, and future business models.

Revenue Management provides visibility into business performance, profitability, growth trends, and financial health.

---

# Objectives

The Revenue Management module shall:

- Track revenue from all sources
- Support revenue recognition
- Support revenue analytics
- Support profitability analysis
- Support revenue forecasting
- Support subscription revenue tracking
- Support marketplace revenue tracking
- Support commission tracking
- Support financial dashboards
- Support future AI revenue forecasting

# Business Rules

1. Every revenue transaction shall have a unique Revenue ID.

2. Revenue shall be categorized.

3. Revenue recognition rules shall be configurable.

4. Revenue allocations shall be auditable.

5. Revenue analytics shall be available.

6. Revenue forecasts shall support future planning.

7. Revenue activities shall be audit logged.

8. Revenue visibility shall be role-based.

9. Revenue configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Revenue Management shall serve as the financial performance and profitability engine of PropertyPilot.
