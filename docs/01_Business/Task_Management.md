# PropertyPilot Task Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Task Management module enables PropertyPilot to create, assign, track, monitor, prioritize, escalate, and complete operational tasks across all business functions.

The module serves as the centralized work management system for agents, vendors, partners, franchises, operations teams, customer support teams, and administrators.

---

# Objectives

The Task Management module shall:

- Create tasks
- Assign tasks
- Track task progress
- Support task prioritization
- Support task escalation
- Support task dependencies
- Support recurring tasks
- Support task analytics
- Support SLA tracking
- Support future workflow automation

# Business Rules

1. Every task shall have a unique Task ID.

2. Tasks shall support configurable workflows.

3. Tasks shall support priorities and due dates.

4. Task assignments shall be traceable.

5. Task escalations shall be configurable.

6. Task activities shall be audit logged.

7. Task visibility shall be role-based.

8. SLA tracking shall be supported.

9. Task configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Task Management shall serve as the centralized work execution and operational task tracking system of PropertyPilot.
