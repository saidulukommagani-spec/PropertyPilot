# PropertyPilot Customer Trust Framework

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the trust principles, controls, mechanisms, and evidence standards used by PropertyPilot.

Objectives:

- Build customer confidence
- Ensure transparency
- Establish evidence credibility
- Protect customer interests
- Differentiate PropertyPilot from competitors

---

# Trust Vision

PropertyPilot shall be recognized as the most trusted property verification and monitoring platform.

Core Promise:

```text
Every report is evidence-backed.

Every visit is traceable.

Every observation is auditable.

Every customer can verify what we verify.
```

# Trust Pillars

## Pillar 1: Verified People

Customers must know:

```text

Who verified

Who approved

Who generated the report
```

## Pillar 2: Verified Evidence

```text

Where it was observed
```

---

## Pillar 3: Transparent Operations

Customers must know:

```text
Current status

Assigned agent

Expected completion date

Report delivery timeline
```

---

## Pillar 4: Auditability

Customers must be able to:

```text
Review visit history

Review evidence

Review reports

Review service activity
```
