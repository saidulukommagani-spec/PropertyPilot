# PropertyPilot Case Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Case Management module enables PropertyPilot to create, manage, investigate, track, resolve, and audit operational, customer, legal, service, vendor, partner, marketplace, and compliance-related cases.

The module serves as the centralized system for managing issues that require structured investigation, collaboration, escalation, approvals, and resolution.

Case Management provides operational visibility, accountability, compliance, and resolution tracking across the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Case Management module shall:

- Manage operational cases
- Support customer issue resolution
- Support investigation workflows
- Support escalations
- Support legal and compliance cases
- Support case assignments
- Support SLA tracking
- Support auditability
- Support cross-functional collaboration
- Support future AI-assisted investigations

# Business Rules

1. Every case shall have a unique Case ID.

2. Cases shall support configurable workflows.

3. Cases may contain evidence and attachments.

4. Case assignments shall be traceable.

5. Escalation rules shall be configurable.

6. SLA tracking shall be supported.

7. Case visibility shall be role-based.

8. All case activities shall be audit logged.

9. Case configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Case Management shall serve as the centralized investigation and resolution platform of PropertyPilot.
