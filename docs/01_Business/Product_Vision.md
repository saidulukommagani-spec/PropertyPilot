# PropertyPilot Product Vision

## Version

1.0

---

# Executive Summary

PropertyPilot is a digital property management and verification platform designed to help property owners, especially Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), remotely manage, verify, monitor, and protect their real estate assets.

The platform provides trusted property services through technology, field operations, verified reports, and a partner ecosystem.

---

# Vision Statement

Enable property owners to manage every stage of the property lifecycle through a single trusted platform.

PropertyPilot aims to become the operating system for property ownership.

---

# Mission Statement

PropertyPilot simplifies property ownership by providing reliable verification, monitoring, reporting, and property management services through a unified digital platform.

---

# Problem Statement

Property owners face several challenges:

```text
Difficulty monitoring properties remotely

Lack of trusted local contacts

Property encroachments

Fraudulent transactions

Unverified ownership records

Construction monitoring challenges

Poor visibility into property condition

Vendor coordination difficulties
```

These challenges are significantly amplified for:

```text
NRIs

Outstation property owners

Senior citizens

Busy professionals

Investors managing multiple properties
```
