# Purpose

The Architecture Index serves as the central navigation document for all PropertyPilot architecture, business, security, data, governance, operations, and decision management artifacts.

This document provides a structured view of the complete enterprise architecture repository and serves as the authoritative entry point for architects, developers, analysts, operations teams, auditors, and business stakeholders.

# Business Rules

1. All architecture documents shall be version controlled.

2. All significant architecture decisions shall be recorded in ADRs.

3. Architecture standards shall be enforced through governance reviews.

4. Repository updates shall follow approved change processes.

5. Architecture artifacts shall remain traceable to business requirements.

6. Architecture documents shall be reviewed periodically.

7. Repository content shall be auditable.

8. Architecture Index shall serve as the authoritative navigation document for the PropertyPilot architecture repository.
