# PropertyPilot Platform Engineering

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Platform Engineering module defines the architecture, standards, processes, tooling, and governance required to provide a scalable, self-service, secure, and developer-friendly internal platform for PropertyPilot engineering teams.

The Platform Engineering capability serves as the foundation for developer productivity, operational consistency, cloud enablement, infrastructure automation, and platform standardization.

---

# Objectives

The Platform Engineering module shall:

- Improve developer productivity
- Standardize platform capabilities
- Enable self-service engineering
- Reduce operational overhead
- Improve deployment consistency
- Accelerate delivery velocity
- Improve platform reliability
- Improve security posture
- Support cloud-native architecture
- Enable enterprise scale operations

---

# Platform Engineering Principles

PropertyPilot Platform Engineering shall be:

- Self-Service First
- Developer Centric
- Platform As Product
- Automation First
- Secure By Design
- Observable
- Reusable
- Scalable
- Reliable
- Governed

# Business Rules

1. Platform capabilities shall be delivered through self-service wherever possible.

2. Infrastructure provisioning shall be automated.

3. Platform services shall comply with architecture standards.

4. Security controls shall be enforced by default.

5. Platform activities shall be audit logged.

6. Platform templates shall be centrally governed.

7. Platform environments shall be standardized.

8. Platform metrics shall be continuously monitored.

9. Platform changes shall follow governance processes.

10. Platform Engineering shall serve as the centralized developer enablement and internal platform framework for PropertyPilot.
