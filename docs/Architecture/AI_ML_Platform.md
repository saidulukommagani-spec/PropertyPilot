## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The AI & ML Platform enables PropertyPilot to build, deploy, manage, monitor, govern, and scale Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning capabilities across all business domains.

The platform serves as the centralized intelligence layer for automation, predictions, recommendations, anomaly detection, optimization, and decision support throughout the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The AI & ML Platform shall:

- Centralize AI capabilities
- Centralize ML model management
- Support predictive analytics
- Support intelligent automation
- Support recommendation engines
- Support anomaly detection
- Support AI governance
- Support model monitoring
- Support responsible AI practices
- Support future Generative AI capabilities

# Business Rules

1. Every AI model shall have a unique Model ID.

2. Models shall support version management.

3. Model validation shall be mandatory before deployment.

4. AI predictions shall be auditable.

5. Model access shall be role-based.

6. AI governance controls shall be enforced.

7. Model performance shall be continuously monitored.

8. Training data shall comply with governance policies.

9. AI configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. AI & ML Platform shall serve as the centralized intelligence and machine learning platform of PropertyPilot.
