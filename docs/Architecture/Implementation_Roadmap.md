# PropertyPilot Implementation Roadmap

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Implementation Roadmap defines the phased delivery strategy for the PropertyPilot platform.

It provides a structured approach for delivering business capabilities, platform services, integrations, analytics, AI capabilities, governance, and operational readiness while minimizing risk and maximizing business value.

---

# Objectives

The roadmap shall:

- Deliver business value incrementally
- Reduce implementation risk
- Enable early customer adoption
- Support scalable growth
- Establish platform foundations first
- Enable continuous delivery
- Support operational readiness
- Align business and technology goals
- Accelerate time to market
- Support future innovation

---

# Business Rules

1. Platform foundation shall be delivered before business modules.

2. Security controls shall be implemented in every phase.

3. Architecture reviews shall be mandatory for major releases.

4. Data governance shall precede enterprise analytics.

5. AI capabilities shall build upon approved data foundations.

6. Operational readiness shall be validated before production rollout.

7. All roadmap changes shall be approved through governance processes.

8. Implementation Roadmap shall serve as the authoritative delivery plan for PropertyPilot.
