# Purpose

The Platform Operations module enables PropertyPilot to manage, operate, monitor, maintain, support, optimize, and govern the day-to-day operations of the entire platform.

The module serves as the centralized operational management framework ensuring platform reliability, availability, performance, scalability, and operational excellence.

---

# Objectives

The Platform Operations module shall:

- Manage platform operations
- Ensure platform availability
- Manage incidents
- Manage operational support
- Manage platform health
- Manage capacity planning
- Manage maintenance activities
- Manage operational governance
- Support operational analytics
- Support continuous improvement

# Business Rules

1. Every operational asset shall have a designated owner.

2. Critical incidents shall follow escalation procedures.

3. Operational activities shall be audit logged.

4. Availability targets shall be monitored continuously.

5. Capacity utilization shall be monitored proactively.

6. Maintenance activities shall follow approval processes.

7. Operational dashboards shall be role-based.

8. Recovery procedures shall be documented and tested.

9. Operational configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Platform Operations shall serve as the centralized operational management framework for PropertyPilot.
