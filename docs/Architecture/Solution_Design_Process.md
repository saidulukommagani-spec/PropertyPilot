## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Solution Design Process defines the standardized methodology, governance, deliverables, approvals, and lifecycle through which business requirements are transformed into production-ready solutions within the PropertyPilot platform.

The process ensures consistency, quality, governance, traceability, security, scalability, and alignment with PropertyPilot enterprise architecture standards.

---

# Objectives

The Solution Design Process shall:

- Standardize solution delivery
- Ensure architecture compliance
- Improve design quality
- Reduce implementation risks
- Improve traceability
- Improve governance
- Improve stakeholder alignment
- Accelerate delivery
- Improve operational readiness
- Support enterprise scalability

# Business Rules

1. Every solution shall originate from an approved business requirement.

2. All solution designs shall undergo architecture review.

3. Security reviews shall be mandatory for critical solutions.

4. Design artifacts shall be version controlled.

5. Solution approvals shall be documented.

6. Testing shall be completed before release approval.

7. Production deployments shall follow release governance processes.

8. Solution activities shall be audit logged.

9. Architecture standards shall be enforced throughout the lifecycle.

10. Solution Design Process shall serve as the authoritative delivery lifecycle framework for PropertyPilot.
