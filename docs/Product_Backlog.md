# PropertyPilot Product Backlog

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Product Backlog serves as the master planning repository for all PropertyPilot features, enhancements, integrations, improvements, and future initiatives.

The backlog helps organize platform development into manageable releases while ensuring alignment with business goals.

---

# Objectives

The Product Backlog shall:

- Organize feature development
- Prioritize business value
- Support phased releases
- Track future enhancements
- Support roadmap planning
- Support scalability planning
- Guide product execution

# Business Rules

1. Every planned feature shall exist in the Product Backlog.

2. Features shall be assigned to releases.

3. Backlog priorities shall be configurable.

4. Product roadmap shall be driven by business value.

5. Release planning shall align with platform strategy.

6. Backlog items shall be tracked through completion.

7. Product Backlog shall serve as the master roadmap for PropertyPilot.
