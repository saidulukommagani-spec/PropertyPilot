# PropertyPilot Marketplace Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Marketplace Management module enables buyers and sellers to discover, list, inquire about, and transact property-related opportunities through the PropertyPilot platform.

The marketplace shall prioritize:

- Trust
- Privacy
- Verification
- Lead Quality
- Property Transparency

PropertyPilot acts as a trusted intermediary between buyers and sellers.

---

# Objectives

The Marketplace shall:

- Enable property listings
- Enable buyer requests
- Enable seller requests
- Protect buyer privacy
- Protect seller privacy
- Support verified properties
- Support verified leads
- Generate marketplace revenue
- Enable future transaction facilitation

# Business Rules

1. Marketplace shall support both buyers and sellers.

2. Buyer and seller privacy shall be protected.

3. Contact sharing shall be configurable.

4. Property verification shall improve trust.

5. Marketplace revenue models shall be configurable.

6. Listings may be verified or unverified.

7. Marketplace activities shall be audit logged.

8. PropertyPilot may act as a trusted intermediary.

9. Marketplace configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Marketplace shall integrate with Property Verification services.
