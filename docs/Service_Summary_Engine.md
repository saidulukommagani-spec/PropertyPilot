# PropertyPilot Service Summary Engine

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Service Summary Engine is responsible for generating concise, easy-to-understand summaries of completed services.

The objective is to provide customers with quick insights without requiring them to review detailed reports, photos, videos, or technical observations.

The Service Summary Engine transforms service evidence and reports into actionable business insights.

---

# Objectives

The Service Summary Engine shall:

- Provide quick service insights
- Improve customer experience
- Simplify report consumption
- Highlight key findings
- Highlight risks and issues
- Recommend next actions
- Support future AI-generated summaries
- Support service upselling opportunities

# Business Rules

1. Every completed service shall generate a summary.

2. Every summary shall be linked to a property report.

3. Every summary shall contain key findings.

4. Every summary shall contain recommendations.

5. Every summary shall support future AI enhancements.

6. Summary templates shall be configurable.

7. Summary activities shall be audit logged.

8. Summary delivery channels shall be configurable.

9. Service summaries shall be customer-friendly and easy to understand.

10. Service summaries are the primary quick-view deliverable of PropertyPilot.
