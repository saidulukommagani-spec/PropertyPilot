# PropertyPilot Expense Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Expense Management module enables PropertyPilot to capture, approve, reimburse, monitor, analyze, and control operational, employee, vendor, franchise, and project-related expenses.

The module serves as the centralized platform for expense governance, cost control, reimbursement processing, budgeting, and financial visibility.

---

# Objectives

The Expense Management module shall:

- Manage expenses
- Support reimbursements
- Support approval workflows
- Support budget controls
- Support expense categorization
- Support cost center allocation
- Support expense analytics
- Support policy compliance
- Support audit requirements
- Support future AI expense intelligence

# Business Rules

1. Every expense shall have a unique Expense ID.

2. Expenses shall support configurable approval workflows.

3. Expense policies shall be configurable.

4. Expense reimbursements shall be trackable.

5. Budget validations shall be supported.

6. Expense activities shall be audit logged.

7. Expense visibility shall be role-based.

8. Financial controls shall be enforced.

9. Expense configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Expense Management shall serve as the centralized expense governance and cost control platform of PropertyPilot.
