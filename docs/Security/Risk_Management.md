# PropertyPilot Risk Management

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Risk Management module enables PropertyPilot to identify, assess, monitor, mitigate, report, and govern risks across customers, properties, services, vendors, partners, franchises, projects, operations, compliance, security, and financial activities.

The module serves as the centralized enterprise risk management framework for the PropertyPilot ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Risk Management module shall:

- Identify risks
- Assess risks
- Monitor risks
- Manage mitigation plans
- Track risk ownership
- Support risk reporting
- Support compliance requirements
- Support audit requirements
- Support risk analytics
- Support future AI risk prediction

# Business Rules

1. Every risk shall have a unique Risk ID.

2. Risks shall support configurable assessment models.

3. Risk mitigation plans shall be trackable.

4. Risk reviews shall be scheduled.

5. Critical risks shall support escalation.

6. Risk activities shall be audit logged.

7. Risk visibility shall be role-based.

8. Risk scoring rules shall be configurable.

9. Risk configuration shall not require code deployment.

10. Risk Management shall serve as the centralized enterprise risk governance framework of PropertyPilot.
