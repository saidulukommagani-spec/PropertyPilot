# Newly Added Screens

| Screen ID | Screen Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| SCR-CUST-033 | KYC Verification | Capture identity information and supporting documents, show verification status, and resolve verification exceptions for customer access to KYC-required services. |
| SCR-CUST-034 | Monitoring Alert Details | Show an alert's severity, evidence, recommended action, and approval controls for the customer to authorise remediation. |
| SCR-CUST-035 | Service Cancellation | Let an eligible customer cancel a service request, supply a reason, and view the resulting cancellation and refund outcome. |
| SCR-CUST-036 | Service Rating | Collect the customer's service rating and feedback after a completed service or vendor-delivered job. |
| SCR-CUST-037 | Family Member Access | Let a property owner grant, review, and revoke family-member access to permitted property information. |
| SCR-CUST-MKT-011 | Protected Property Interaction | Present the mediated buyer-seller interaction, coordination status, and protected-contact rules without exposing direct contact details. |
| SCR-AGT-015 | Mobile Service Checklist | Guide an assigned agent through the mandatory, service-specific checklist before job completion. |
| SCR-AGT-016 | Offline Sync Status | Show queued field evidence and reports, retry synchronisation, and surface conflicts that require agent resolution. |
| SCR-NRI-006 | Emergency Alert Detail and Response | Let an NRI customer inspect emergency evidence and choose or approve the recommended next action. |
