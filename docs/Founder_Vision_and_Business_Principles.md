# Founder Vision and Business Principles

## Purpose

This document captures the founder's vision, business philosophy,
core principles, marketplace strategy, revenue philosophy,
pricing philosophy and long-term direction for PropertyPilot.

This document is the primary business reference for PropertyPilot.

In case of conflict between business requirements and technical design,
this document takes precedence.

All major business decisions must be reflected here.

---

# Vision

PropertyPilot is an end-to-end Property Lifecycle Management Platform
that helps customers buy, sell, rent, verify, monitor, protect,
maintain, construct and manage properties through a single trusted platform.

Our goal is:

"One Platform for Every Property Need"

---

# Mission

Provide end-to-end property services through a trusted,
technology-driven platform while protecting customer interests
and simplifying property ownership.

---

# What PropertyPilot Is

PropertyPilot is:

- Property Lifecycle Platform
- Property Services Platform
- Property Marketplace
- Lead Brokerage Platform
- Subscription Platform
- Property Ownership Ecosystem

---

# What PropertyPilot Is Not

PropertyPilot is not a simple property monitoring application.

PropertyPilot is not a lead listing website.

PropertyPilot is not a vendor directory.

PropertyPilot is not a classifieds platform.

PropertyPilot is a managed property ecosystem where all interactions
are facilitated through the platform.

# Property Ownership Principle

A property may have:

- Single Owner
- Multiple Owners
- Family Ownership
- Partnership Ownership

# Service Aggregation Principle

PropertyPilot does not need to execute every service.

PropertyPilot may:

- Deliver the service
- Coordinate the service
- Manage the service
- Aggregate vendors
- Facilitate transactions

The customer experience remains owned by PropertyPilot.
# Customer Cost Discovery Principle

Customers should be able to estimate service costs
before registration.

The platform should provide:

"Check Your Property Service Cost"

which compares:

- One-Time Service Cost
- Bundle Cost
- Subscription Cost
- Estimated Savings
# Marketplace Philosophy

- Buy Property
- Sell Property

- Security

# Vendor Neutral Principle

PropertyPilot is vendor agnostic.

Customers purchase services from PropertyPilot.

- Location
- Availability
- Quality Score
- Pricing
- Performance History

# Core Business Philosophy

## Trusted Intermediary Model

PropertyPilot acts as the trusted intermediary between all parties.

### Buy & Sell

Buyer
↓
PropertyPilot
↓
Seller

For Buyer:
PropertyPilot = Seller Representative

For Seller:
PropertyPilot = Buyer Representative

### Rental

Tenant
↓
PropertyPilot
↓
Owner

For Tenant:
PropertyPilot = Owner Representative

For Owner:
PropertyPilot = Tenant Representative

### Service Marketplace

Customer
↓
PropertyPilot
↓
Vendor

For Customer:
PropertyPilot = Service Provider

For Vendor:
PropertyPilot = Customer Representative
