# PropertyPilot Revenue Data Model

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Revenue Data Model defines the structure, relationships, lifecycle, validation rules, governance standards, and business rules for revenue management within the PropertyPilot platform.

This model serves as the authoritative source for revenue generation, billing, invoicing, collections, commissions, recurring income, financial forecasting, and revenue analytics.

---

# Objectives

The Revenue Data Model shall:

- Manage revenue lifecycle
- Track revenue sources
- Support invoicing and billing
- Manage collections
- Support recurring revenue
- Enable financial reporting
- Support revenue forecasting
- Improve financial visibility
- Support multi-tenant operations
- Enable AI-driven revenue insights

# Revenue Recognition Rules

- Revenue shall not be recognized before the applicable event occurs.
- Revenue recognition shall be auditable.
- Revenue adjustments shall retain history.

---

# Collection Rules

- Partial payments shall be supported.
- Overdue invoices shall generate alerts.
- Collections shall update invoice status automatically.

---

# Commission Rules

- Commission amounts shall be calculated from approved revenue.
- Commission payments shall be traceable.
- Commission history shall be retained permanently.

# Business Rules

1. Every revenue record shall belong to a tenant.

2. Revenue codes shall be unique within a tenant.

3. Revenue lifecycle status shall be maintained.

4. Revenue recognition shall be auditable.

5. Invoices shall support multiple line items.

6. Revenue collections shall support partial payments.

7. Revenue forecasts shall be maintained separately.

8. Soft delete shall be used for revenue records.

9. Financial information shall be protected.

10. Revenue Data Model shall serve as the authoritative revenue data structure for PropertyPilot.
